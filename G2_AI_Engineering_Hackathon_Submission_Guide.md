# G2 AI Engineering Hackathon
## Participant Submission Guide

Step-by-step instructions for registering and submitting your project on HackerEarth.

> **Source:** Converted from the official G2 AI Engineering Hackathon Participant Submission Guide PDF.
>
> **Note:** The original PDF includes screenshots of the HackerEarth submission UI. This Markdown version preserves the textual instructions and structure; the screenshots themselves are not embedded.

---

## Submission Flow

**Register -> Start Submission -> Create Submission -> Complete Mandatory Fields -> Submit**

---

## Step 1 - Access the microsite & Start Submission

On the Hackathon microsite, you'll see the option **Start submission**.

- **Start submission:** Click this to begin your project submission process.

---

## Step 2 - Open My Dashboard

When you click **Start submission**, you will be taken to your **My Dashboard** for the hackathon.

Here you can view your:

- Team information
- Registration status
- Submission progress
- Submission area

---

## Step 3 - Create a New Submission

In the **Submissions** section, you will initially see **No submissions yet**.

Click **+ New Submission** to create your project submission.

**Visual cue in the original PDF:** The "+ New Submission" button is highlighted in the screenshot.

---

## Step 4 - Fill in the Submission Form

The **Create submission** form will open. Complete all fields marked with an **asterisk (*)** because they are mandatory.

### Mandatory fields shown in the guide

- **Title:** Enter a clear and descriptive title for your project.
- **Description:** Describe your project, solution, or idea. Formatting and links can be used where required.
- **Presentation:** Upload your pitch deck/slides.

### Accepted presentation formats

- `.key`
- `.odp`
- `.odt`
- `.pdf`
- `.pps`
- `.ppt`
- `.pptx`

**Maximum presentation file size:** 50 MB

---

## Step 5 - Complete the Form & Submit

Complete the remaining mandatory fields and then click **Submit**. Your submission will only be officially received after you click **Submit**.

### Remaining mandatory fields

- **Repository URL:** Add the link to your code repository, such as GitHub or Bitbucket.
- **Source Code:** Upload your source code (for example, `.zip` or `.apk`).
- **Instructions to Run:** Add clear steps so reviewers can run and test your project.

### Upload limit

**Maximum file size:** 50 MB for each applicable upload field, including the Presentation and Source Code.

### Important submission instruction

Do **not** select **Save as Draft** if you intend to submit. Click **Submit** to complete the submission.

### Final check before submitting

Make sure that:

- Your repository link is correct.
- Your source code is uploaded.
- Your presentation is uploaded.
- Your instructions to run the project are complete.
- All mandatory fields have been filled.

---
