Why product preservation is deterministic

  1. The approved product cutout is human-verified and traceable by SHA-256 to the reference photo.
  2. Every fully opaque cutout pixel is compared against the final composite: 310,136 pixels, 0 mismatches in all 20 outputs.
  3. Pixel identity is authoritative for the preserved product pixels. When the AI evaluator disagrees (for example, by misreading the stylized "COMET" insole print), the disagreement is kept as a recorded observation.
